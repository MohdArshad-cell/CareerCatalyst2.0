# LinkedIn SEO: Ranking #1 for Top Roles

The biggest mistake professionals make on LinkedIn is treating it like an online resume. 

LinkedIn is not a resume repository. **LinkedIn is a search engine.**

<Callout>
When a technical recruiter at Google, Meta, or a high-growth startup needs to fill a role, they don't post a job and wait for applications. They open a tool called **LinkedIn Recruiter Pro** and run boolean search queries.
</Callout>

If your profile doesn't contain the exact keywords they are searching for, in the exact places the algorithm prioritizes, you will never show up in their results.

## The Algorithm Priority List

LinkedIn's algorithm weighs keywords differently depending on where they appear on your profile. Here is the hierarchy of importance:

1. **Job Titles** (Highest Weight)
2. **Headline**
3. **Skills Section**
4. **About / Summary**
5. **Experience Bullets** (Lowest Weight)

### 1. Fixing Your Headline

Your headline is prime real estate. Most people just put their current title:
> `Manager at Company X`

This is terrible for SEO. You are wasting 220 characters of searchable text. Instead, use the **Pipeline Formula**:
> `Current Title | Keyword 1 | Keyword 2 | Keyword 3 | Impact Statement`

**Example:**
> `Senior Professional at Startup | Strategy | Leadership | Operations | Building scalable operations for $1M+ revenue`

Now, you are indexing for "Strategy", "Leadership", "Operations", and "Scaling". 

### 2. The Skills Section is Not Optional

Recruiters filter their searches by checking boxes for specific skills. If a recruiter checks the box for "Agile" and you only mention Agile in your "About" section but NOT in your official "Skills" section, you will be filtered out.

Max out your 50 skills. Pin your top 3 tech stack skills to the very top.

## Stop Writing Paragraphs

No one reads paragraphs on the internet, especially not recruiters scanning 500 profiles a day.

Break your "About" section into easily scannable sections:
- **TL;DR:** A one-sentence summary of who you are.
- **Core Competencies:** A bulleted list of the tools and methodologies you use.
- **What I Do:** 3 bullet points of your biggest accomplishments (using the XYZ framework).

## Inbound is Better Than Outbound

When your profile is perfectly optimized, you shift from "Outbound" (cold applying to portals and getting rejected) to "Inbound" (recruiters messaging you with interview requests).
